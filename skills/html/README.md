# /html — pages instead of walls of text

Claude hands you a page you open in a browser: the answer on top, the proof beside each claim, your open questions in an amber panel at the end.
The first time you use it, Claude offers a short interview (`ONBOARD.md`) to tune the pages to you; skip it and the defaults work.
`examples/system.html` shows every building block, live, in dark and light.
Try this: "/html compare the three ways we could add sign-in to my app, and tell me which you'd pick."
